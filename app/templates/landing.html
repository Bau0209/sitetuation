How It Works

Three steps to your ideal location

No spreadsheets. No consultants. Just enter an address and let SiteTuation do the work.

01

Select a Location

Enter any Philippine address into the SiteTuation search bar. The map instantly pins your location and loads all available geographic context.

02

Analyze the Data

The platform analyzes population density, crime rates, disaster risk levels, nearby competitors, foot traffic, and infrastructure — all at once.

03

Get Smart Recommendations

Our AI generates a risk score and a clear, easy-to-read report so you can make confident, data-backed business decisions.

Features

Everything you need
to choose wisely

🏙️

Population & Demographics

Access city and barangay-level population figures, consumer density, and age profiles to understand your potential customer base before committing.

🛡️

Crime & Safety Index

Incident data is overlaid directly on the map so you can see safety conditions at a glance — not just a number, but context you can act on.

🌊

Disaster Risk Levels

Flood zones, earthquake fault lines, and typhoon exposure data are integrated from government sources and displayed per location automatically.

🏪

Competitor Mapping

See nearby businesses in your industry, their distance, and market saturation. Understand whether the area is underserved or crowded before you sign a lease.

✨

AI Summary & Recommendations

After analysis, our AI synthesizes all data points into a human-readable summary with a risk score and an actionable recommendation — so you spend less time interpreting and more time deciding. Available on Plus and Pro plans.
